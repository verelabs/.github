## Making things is hard. Proving it should not be.

A finished piece hides everything that went into it: the false starts, the ninety sessions, the version deleted at 2am. Then someone in the comments says "AI". Or a client asks what the hours were for. Or you want to share how something was made without handing over the thing itself.

Vere Labs builds tools that let work vouch for itself: records of how a thing was made that nobody can backdate, that reveal exactly as much as the maker chooses, and that anyone can check without trusting us.

## Two projects, two sides of one idea

**[velender](https://github.com/verelabs/velender) shows the work.** A Blender add-on that turns a project's history into a verifiable receipt: anchored snapshots, a timelapse, a stat card, and a page any skeptic can verify in their own browser. Prove art is hard work.

**[enclave](https://github.com/verelabs/enclave) protects the work.** A private, real-time, AI-first markdown workspace where every AI run is recorded and the document's timeline can be anchored so nothing is backdated. Provenance is its own grantable layer: a blogger can hand a fact-checker the label (which passages a model drafted, with what prompts, edited how, and when) without ever showing the draft. The server sees none of it.

## What we hold to

- Effort is evidence, and progression is stronger evidence than hours.
- Proof should not cost privacy. Reveal the receipt, keep the work.
- Verification should not need us. Public anchors, open formats, checks that run on the reader's machine.
